A season lasts three months, ending where the world resets. How it works:

- **When the world turns over** — it resets when its own weight, storage, demands it. After the first, every three months.
- **What is kept** — staff triage and preserve what stands as notable; Create schematics are exported wherever broader preservation is warranted.
- **What a season leaves you** — reward and memory are the same mechanic: you choose, from your own hands, what you carry forward, proven by reading your own inventory file the moment the season closes. How much you may carry is shaped by how present you were: `1 + (Playtime / Average Playtime)`, capped at 3.
- **The active are given a head start**; the casual are never punished for playing casually — the base is always theirs.

notable events, and who was around.
