*How the community governs itself — managed democracy, what may be put to a
vote, how a vote is won, and the one condition that gives pause. Power here
is described not by what it commands, but by what it chooses to restrain.*

## Managed Democracy

Not a true democracy, and not meant to be one:

- The players hold real weight — through vote, through voice, through suggestion.
- The Warden keeps one hand free, to step in only where the outcome turns oppressive or harmful.
- The great powers of the world govern their own domains, their own laws, layered beneath what the community votes into being above them.
- Staff stays out of what belongs to the players — unless drawn in directly, by alliance, by direct action, or by oppression.

### What May Be Put to a Vote

- Staff removal — a vote of no confidence only, never the choosing of staff, which stays with the Wayfinder application and the Oathsworn's handpicking
- Budget and spending
- The shape of the world itself
- Community-wide law
- A mod's approval
- A mod's removal, where the cause is simply that the people no longer want it
- General policy

**What a vote decides, stands.** The outcome is binding — the Warden carries
it out — unless it strikes the one condition that gives pause: oppression, or
harm.

### Who May Raise Their Voice

A player earns their vote by meeting every one of these:

- A linked Roblox account
- Having set foot on the server at least once
- Thirty minutes or more of playtime
- Twenty-four hours or more as a member

Those who founded this place carry their voice regardless, until the world
itself leaves its alpha behind.

**Not every voice carries the same weight** — Kinfolk and Artisans speak with
**twice** the weight of any other eligible voice.

**Any player may call a vote into being.** No permission is needed to ask the
question.

| Category | Threshold |
| - | - |
| The weightiest matters (staff removal, budget) | 60% supermajority |
| Everything else (mods, the world's shape, general policy) | Simple majority (>50%) |

**A vote that no one attends decides nothing** — forty percent of the
eligible must turn out, or the result does not stand.

**The Warden does not vote in the ordinary matters** of the people — only
where leadership itself, or the weightiest of proceedings, calls for its
voice.

**A staff member removed by no confidence** may yet return — but only if the
Warden itself judges it so. Never automatic. Never forever barred.

## When a Vote Strikes the One Condition

Should a binding vote's outcome trip the line of oppression or harm:

1. The matter goes first to the review panel — not to the Warden.
2. The Warden acts only where the **panel agrees** the intervention is warranted.
3. No unilateral hand reaches in here — the same burden the owner carries on the appeals side, so power is checked coming and going, never resting easy in one place alone.

## When Staff Steps In

Staff intervenes where:

- Oppression is being done, by one or by many
- A community-voted law is being broken
- Direct action is needed — an alliance, a dispute that has crossed a line

## Sovereignty of the Player

- Whoever wants land, a people, a stake — may claim it, unclaimed by any hand above them.
- Disputes and fights belong to those in them, unless oppression is found within them.
- Staff and the Oathsworn stay clear of it, unless drawn in directly.

## Tiers of Service

The roles of this world stand final — settled, not subject to reshaping.

- **The Warden** — the founder, the owner of the world, the only hand that holds OP unmanaged, unwatched, the most present of all who serve. Authority wrapped in a leash they keep on themselves: every override explained, every judgment justified, every claim to power answerable to the people it protects.
- **The Pathfinder** — the lead advisor, walking beside the Warden rather than behind. Theirs is the seat on the Review Panel; their clarity comes from distance from the fray.
- **The Oathsworn** — handpicked, never applied for, trusted with a narrow blade: `say`, `kick`, and their kind — never `op`, `ban`, `gamemode`, or `give`. Every command passes through a custom proxy that checks shape and permission and logs every word.
- **The Panelists** — four seats held by the community's own, drawn from Kinfolk and Artisan alike and returned to the vote each month.
- **The Artisans** — the contributors, the makers and shapers; the primary channel through which new mods and features are felt out. Their votes carry double weight.
- **The Kinfolk** — the supporters (booster, VIP, or other privileged member), carrying double votes on community decisions. Not staff — no traditional staff access.
- **The Wayfinders** — the lowest tier of staff and the first face a newcomer meets: tech support, simple to in-between FAQ, casual engagement. They do not warn or ban; chosen by application, never by a single hand alone, proven over two weeks.
- **The Settlers** — every player who plants a flag and calls this place home. No title, no privilege beyond belonging.

How they are chosen, how they may be reached, and how they are held to
account:

- **Wayfinders** — by application, then staff's own review and decision, with the community's voice weighed in. Proven over two weeks.
- **Oathsworn** — sought out by the Warden on loyalty, resourcefulness, respect, maturity, professionalism, and compassion. Never forced; the oath is offered, and they choose to carry it. Two strikes — the first a written warning, the second the role itself returned.
- **Pathfinders and the Warden** — earned through a trust built the quiet way; never seized, never inherited.
- **Panelists** — a seat earned by vote each month, held only as long as trust lasts.

The doors that stand open to reach them: `#report-player-conduct` (a player's
behavior, reported by any voice), `#report-technical-bugs` (the live reporting
channel), `#bug-reports` (the static record kept during a mod's alpha week),
`#report-staff-complaints` (the subject never sees the reports against
themselves), and `#ban-appeals` (the door to the Review Panel).

Appeals and the Review Panel are defined in [the Rules](../rules/).
