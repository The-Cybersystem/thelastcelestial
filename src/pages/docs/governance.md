> **Placeholder.** Describe how decisions are made, who holds operator power,
> and how you can appeal.

- **Operators** hold the keys with the blessing of the admin.
- **Lore changes** go through pull requests on this repository.
- **Modpack changes** are staged in `mods-pending/` and tested before they
  reach the live server, which is protected by a signed integrity baseline.
- **Player disputes** that the rules don't cover get resolved as *rulings* and
  are recorded in the [archive](../../archive/rulings/).
