*The law of the world — where staff may reach, how judgment is weighed, the
right to be heard again, what is owed between players, how land is held and
war declared, and the integrity that holds the world together. What is owed
and what is forbidden lives here. Power here is described not by what it
commands, but by what it chooses to restrain.*

## On the Reach of Staff

A domain governs itself. What happens within its borders — feuds settled,
harm weighed as story or as wound — is not staff's to name. That line,
between the tale and the true hurt behind it, belongs to the people living
it, and no Warden or Wayfinder draws it for them.

Staff steps in only when the matter stops being the domain's alone:

- Oppression of a player or a people
- The breaking of a rule the community itself voted into being
- An exploit or dupe that threatens the world entire
- Doxxing
- A threat that reaches past the screen
- Conduct that spills into the Discord halls

Everywhere else, the domain rules the domain.

## How Judgment Is Weighed

There is no ladder to climb, no chart to consult. Every case is heard fresh,
and weighed against four things alone:

- **Intent**
- **Severity and harm**
- **What came before**
- **Remorse, and the willingness to make it right**

This is the standard, and it does not bend for the categories of wrongdoing —
even a dupe found mid-alpha is judged by what was meant and what it cost, not
punished on reflex.

A narrow few offenses stand outside this weighing entirely — named in the
Hard Rules, marked **zero tolerance**, they carry their consequence the
moment they are confirmed. One further class, the **server felony**, still
passes through the four factors, but is never permitted to land soft: a
confirmed felony cannot end in a mere warning.

Felonies do not wait on a panel's word. Staff may act the moment judgment is
reached — the Review Panel remains open to them after, as it does to any
ruling, but never before.

### Who May Speak Up

Any voice may raise a report — the wronged and the witness alike. No one need
be the one who bled to say that something bled.

### What Is Shown

Every ruling is written down. What can be shared without exposing the people
inside it is shared — a redacted account, given to the community as its due.

## The Right to Be Heard Again

A ruling is not the end of the matter. It may be carried to the **Review
Panel** — a body apart from whoever first passed judgment.

**Seven seats sit the panel, an odd number by design, so no vote ever splits
the room in two:**

- The community's owner, the Warden
- Its Pathfinder
- Two supporters, chosen by election
- Two contributors, chosen by election
- One seat held by whoever gave the world the most of their hours this past
  month — a seat the rest of the panel fills first, before the standing
  itself decides it

The elected seats turn over **monthly**. A ruling stands or falls on
**simple majority** — four of seven.

**The owner's hand remains over all of it — but it is a hand kept still more
often than raised.** Override is invoked only when the owner believes, in
earnest, that the panel's judgment was compromised, tilted, or unjust — and
never without a reason spoken plainly to the whole community first. Outside
that narrow door, the panel's word is final, and the owner honors it as such.

The letter of this clause is left open on purpose — better a principle the
community trusts than a checklist the clever learn to slip past. These stand
as **illustrative, not exhaustive**:

- **Conflict of interest** — a judge too close to the judged.
- **Vote-trading, or votes cast in concert rather than conscience.**
- **A ruling whose weight matches nothing in the case laid before it.**
- **Process abandoned** — no quorum met, no voice heard from the accused, a
  seat excluded improperly.
- **A panel bent by pressure from outside it** — threat, bribe, or a crowd
  too loud to ignore.
- **A pattern, not a moment** — a judge who always rules the same way against
  the same name, whatever the case in front of them.

**Even the untouchable can ask to be heard again.** The zero-tolerance
rulings — doxxing, evasion, fraud, cheating, the banned content — may still
be appealed, but only on the ground of *process*: the wrong hand accused, a
mistake in the finding. The tolerance itself is not on trial.

## What Is Owed Between Players

A floor of respect holds everywhere — in the world and across the bridge to
Discord alike.

- No hatred spoken, no slur, no harassment aimed at a person (Hard Rules 4, 5).
- This is a managed democracy: the people shape the server by voice and vote, and the Warden steps in only when that shaping turns to harm.
- What is written here is not carved in stone — it grows as the community votes new rules into it. But nothing voted in unmakes what stands zero-tolerance in the Hard Rules.

## Land, and the Wars Fought Over It

- **Sovereignty is real.** Whoever wants land, a people, and a stake of their own may have it — no hand from above claims what a player has claimed first.
- **Disputes belong to those having them.** Staff stays clear unless a cross-domain trigger from the Reach of Staff is struck, or an alliance draws them in directly.
- **Idle combat carries no server-wide law** — each domain writes its own (Hard Rules 3).
- **War between domains answers to one rule alone: it must be declared** (Hard Rules 2).

## The Integrity of the World

- No dupe, no glitch, no exploit turned to gain. What is found broken is reported, not spent.
- This stands apart from sanctioned alpha-testing, where the same four-factor weighing applies to what's uncovered.
- A verification system watches the world:
  - To ensure a cheat-free environment and protect our economy, our server uses an automated verification system to check the mods and client files (.jar files) you use when connecting.
  - It reads nothing from memory — only the files you connect with are checked, and nothing is hidden about what is.
- **To join is to consent** — every player is told, plainly, that they are testing, and that their files are checked.

A false flag has never been seen.

## What Testing Asks of You

- To play here is to test, by design — bugs and glitches are meant to be surfaced, never buried.
- Every client mod must be CurseForge-verified. Nothing unverified, nothing suspicious, finds its way in — the threat of malice hiding in a mod file is real, and the posture here stays strict for it.
- These are guides, not gates carved in iron. What matters most is that the world still holds together.

## The Hard Rules

What each mark means:

- 🔴 **Zero tolerance** — no weighing, the consequence is automatic.
- 🟠 **Server felony** — still weighed, but never lightly.
- 🟡 **Standard** — judged like anything else, by the four factors.
- ⚙️ **Automod** — the machine's word, not staff's.
- ⚪ **No baseline** — the domain's to decide, not the server's.
