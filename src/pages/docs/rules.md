A short, hard-mode world runs on shared trust. Character of the rules:

- The world runs **Survival · Hard**. Death hurts.
- **PVP** is enabled — settle it out of character before you settle it in it.
- **No cheating the modpack.** Runtime manipulation or deviant modlists trigger
  the integrity service; expect removal.
- **No griefing player bases** without consent. Build-shrink, claims and theft
  are handled by [governance](../governance/).
- Pranks are lore. Vandalism isn't.
