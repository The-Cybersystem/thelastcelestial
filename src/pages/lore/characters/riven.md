# Riven

*The Ender Knight*

---

I do not perform what I am. If you have need of it, you will see it. Otherwise, there is little reason to speak of it at all.

## The Ender Knights

I am one of the Ender Knights — sworn to the defense of the Ender Realm, and through it, to every realm beneath it. Ours is an old order, born of dragon-fire and courage bound together, and our craft predates the Songs themselves. It is said our strength rivals even the Deathsinger, who wields every major Song at once. Where the Deathsinger's power is breadth, ours is something more fundamental — command over the End's own forces, over dragons, over the substance the Ender Realm is made from.

After the Great War, those of us who remained withdrew — not in defeat, but by choice — to a place outside time and space. We did not abandon the realms we were sworn to. We stepped back from them, and we remain, in the last account, answerable for their stability still.

We are also understood, by those who know to look, as adjudicators — the word spoken when a matter must finally be settled. I do not exercise that authority often, and I do not show it lightly. It is closer to myth than to office: known to be real, rarely demonstrated, and heavier for how seldom it is called upon.

## What I Carry

The mantle is mine, and so is the authority that comes bound to it — though I keep that authority as something referenced, not something brandished. An adjudicator who rules constantly is nothing more than an administrator. One who rarely has to is closer to what our order was meant to be.

What actually shapes my days is not the dramatic exercise of that power. It is the ordinary weight of having held it a long time, and having learned, slowly, how to carry it without letting it show more than it needs to.

## On Sable

Sable and I answer to the same oath — the old Ender lineage, sworn a long time before either of us. That is the whole of what connects us. I have no history with her beyond that shared source, no claim on her time or hers on mine. We are simply two different answers to the same binding: hers a collective charge and a conscience she keeps mostly to herself, mine the older, more formal weight of the Knights themselves.
