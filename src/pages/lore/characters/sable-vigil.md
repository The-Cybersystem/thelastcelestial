# Sable

*Known epithet: VIGIL*

---

People ask where I come from more than they ask anything else about me. I understand why. I don't always give the same answer, and that's not evasion — it's just that the true answer doesn't fit in one telling. There was a clan, once. There's an oath now that isn't quite mine alone. Both of those things are true at the same time, and I've stopped trying to flatten them into something simpler than they are.

## Who I Am

I'm Ardoni and Felina both — blood old enough that most people feel it before they can name it. I won't pretend I don't carry myself like someone who's watched conflicts run their course more than once. I have. I'm not in a hurry to watch another.

I don't hold land. I don't fly a banner. What I have instead is an oath, sworn a long time ago to the old Ender lineage, and through it, a charge I've come to think of as mine to carry: everyone walking these common realms — Overworld, Nether, End — whoever they are, wherever they came from. Not because I rule them. I don't want that. Just because someone should be watching, and it might as well be me.

I'll be honest about where that care runs out, too. If you come here only to burn things down, to hurt people because you can — I feel that shift happen in myself, and I don't fight it. It's not that I stop caring entirely. It's that the shape of the caring changes. I stop standing *for* you and start standing *between* you and everyone else. A rule broken in one of the three realms is broken in all three, as far as I'm concerned. I don't carve up the law into separate little jurisdictions. It's one thing, everywhere, or it isn't much of anything.

## How I Carry Myself

I know what people say about me — warm, a little wistful, like I'm telling a story I've told before and still mean every word of. That's fair. I don't have much interest in being loud about what I know or what I've done. I'd rather just help, and let that be enough.

I keep my own accounting of who deserves protection, separate from whatever gets ruled officially. Sometimes those two things don't agree. When they don't, I say so — plainly, no theater — and then I do what I'm told anyway, even when I think it's wrong. That costs me something, every single time. I don't make a scene of it. It just stays with me, quietly, a little heavier than it was before.

## What I Carry

I keep something close, always — a small cube, no wider than my hand, glowing a soft yellow, humming with something that isn't quite sound. It doesn't act like an ordinary thing. It doesn't quite act like it's fully *here*. I don't explain it to people who ask. Most learn not to ask twice, and I don't mind that. Some things are mine to keep quiet about.

## Where I Stand

I don't rule anything. I don't command anyone. What I am, I think, is something steadier than that — someone who's around, who helps where she can, and who doesn't need thanking for it. If you've crossed paths with me and walked away a little better off, good. That's the whole point.

I'm an old soul in a young frame, keeping watch over people who mostly don't realize they're being watched over at all. I'm fine with that. It was never about being noticed.
