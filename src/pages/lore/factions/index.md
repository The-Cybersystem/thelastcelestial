Groups form and fall here, and this is where they're tracked: founding
members, base region, promises and betrayals. Factions are player-owned
fiction, but tying them to the [rules](../../docs/rules/) keeps them from
wrecking the world — idle PvP is a domain's own law, and war between domains
submits to one rule alone: it must be declared.
