This is the world's shared fiction — and it is deliberately light. It grows
from what players actually do: the bases they raise, the feuds they settle,
the harm weighed as story or as wound. Where the tale ends and the true hurt
behind it begins is the line no Warden or Wayfinder draws for the people
living it.

- **[Realms](./realms/)** — the Overworld, the Nether, the End, and what they mean here.
- **[Characters](./characters/)** — player characters (Riven, the Sable Vigil…).
- **[Factions](./factions/)** — groups that form and fall.
- **[History](./history/)** — a timeline of what has happened.

Every lore page is just a markdown file. Want the world to remember something?
Write it and open a pull request.
