Refined netherite and long vaults dominate the Nether — dangerous, valuable,
and routinely contested. Refining there draws heat: the farther the risk, the
finer the return. What matters to the world: the built highways between
faction reaches, the fortresses converted into forward bases, and who
controls the pathways — the record will be kept here as it happens.
