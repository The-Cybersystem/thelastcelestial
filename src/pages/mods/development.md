> **Placeholder.**

- **Staging** — new mods land in the server's `mods-pending/` folder first,
  are tested, and only then promoted.
- **Baseline** — the signed integrity baseline is generated by a blessed
  client, never by the server. Server-only mods (like `bluemap`) are recorded
  in `config/integrity-server.json` → `serverOnlyModAllowlist`.
- **Site changes** — this repository: push to `main`, and GitHub Actions
  rebuilds and publishes automatically.
