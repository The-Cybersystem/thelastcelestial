*To play here is to test, by design — bugs and glitches are meant to be
surfaced, never buried.*

## A Mod's Path to the World

1. **Proposed** — by a player, or by a voice of trust.
2. **Supported** — by the people, or by those voices of trust.
3. **Audited** — for dupes and glitches.
4. **Weighed** — by senior staff for fit with the world.
5. **Checked** — against the ~two hundred mods already standing.
6. **Proven** — given one week in the wild before it earns its place.

Every client mod must be **CurseForge-verified** — the threat of malice
hiding in a mod file is real, so nothing unverified finds its way in.

These are guides, not gates carved in iron. What matters most is that the
world still holds together.

## The Turning of the Week

| Day | Activity |
| - | - |
| Sunday – Monday | Suggestions open, voting begins |
| Tuesday | Final checks, approval given |
| Wednesday | Brought into the world |
| Thursday – Friday | Verified against the mod site |
| Weekend | Ready to be played |

## How a Mod Is Unmade

Two roads, not one:

- **The safety valve** — staff or the Warden may pull a broken or exploitable mod alone, at once, no vote required. An emergency answered as one.
- **The people's will** — where the cause is simply popularity lost, the standard community vote decides it.

## What Is Built From Nothing

- Custom mods are raised **from scratch**, to avoid the tangle of dependency conflicts.
- Built specifically to belong within this ~200-mod world.
