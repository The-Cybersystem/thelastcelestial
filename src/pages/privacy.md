# Privacy Policy

*Version 1.0 — last updated 2026-09-22*

This policy explains what information we handle for three connected services, and nothing here tries to hide anything from you. We operate from the United States.

## Short version

- The website is a static page: no cookies, no accounts, no analytics.
- The Minecraft server records the basics needed to run a game — usernames, UUIDs, game logs (including chat and connection data) — and verifies your game files when you join.
- The Discord community is governed first by Discord's own Terms of Service and privacy policy. On top of that, we keep the moderation and safety records we need, and we run accounts and automation that Discord permits.
- We share nothing with anyone for money. Third-party services are used only in the categories described below.

## What we process

### Website

The site is static and public. It sets no cookies, holds no accounts, runs no analytics, and has no comment system. Standard technical logs created while serving the site are handled by our hosting provider under its own policies.

### Minecraft server

To run the game and protect the world, we process:

- **Identifiers** — your username and UUID.
- **Game logs** — chat, connections, and related technical records that keep the server running and enforceable under the [Rules](./docs/rules/).
- **File verification** — as the [Rules](./docs/rules/) describe, an automated check reviews the game files (.jar files) you connect with, to keep the world cheat-free.
- **The live map** — shows the world without player positions.

### Discord community

The Discord server is governed first by [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy): Discord itself collects and handles data about your use of the platform under its own terms, which we don't control.

On top of that, we maintain the data we need to run and moderate the community safely:

- Member records, and the moderation and safety activity of the server.
- Our own accounts and automation — including bots and dashboard controls with administrator-level access, as Discord permits — which may log, moderate, and act to keep the community safe. Where a system needs to record messages or activity to do that job, it does.
- These records are retained as long as needed for the purposes below — including indefinitely where community safety or legal compliance requires it.

## Third-party services

We don't sell data. We rely on a small number of service providers, described here by category, each bound by its own terms:

- **Hosting** — web, storage, and game hosting required to run the services.
- **Live map and content delivery** — serving the world map and site content.
- **Account linking** — connecting a Minecraft account to a Discord account through a third-party service.
- **Identity verification and community-safety review** — verifying that people are who they say they are, and helping identify accounts tied to harmful or illegal conduct.
- **Anti-abuse and moderation automation** — tools that watch for and respond to abuse so moderators can keep the community safe.

Where one of these providers receives data, it receives only what it needs to perform its role.

## How we use information

To operate the services; to keep them secure and cheat-free; to moderate and enforce the [Rules](./docs/rules/) and [Terms](./terms/); to prevent, detect, and respond to harmful or illegal conduct; and to comply with the law.

## Your rights

You can reach us at any time at [riven.thefoundation@proton.me](mailto:riven.thefoundation@proton.me) or by messaging [Riven Webster](https://discord.com/users/738434699021778945). We respond to every request and act on it within a reasonable time.

- **United States.** You may ask to see, correct, or delete the data we hold about you. If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act and the California Privacy Rights Act give you the right to know what we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of any sale or sharing of your data — we do not sell or share it.
- **European Economic Area (GDPR).** We rely primarily on the legitimate interests of operating, securing, and moderating our services. You have the right to access, correct, delete, and restrict or object to processing of your data, and to receive the data in a portable form. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Where data is transferred to the United States, we rely on appropriate safeguards and standard protections.
- **United Kingdom (UK GDPR).** The same rights apply under the UK regime. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

## Age

Our services are meant for people at least 13 years old, in line with Discord's Terms of Service and data-protection expectations. We do not knowingly process data from anyone under 13. If you're under 13, please don't use the services or give us any personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has done so, contact us and we'll take reasonable steps to remove it.

## Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect what we hold. Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy — including indefinitely where community safety, anti-abuse, or legal compliance requires it. When a purpose no longer applies, we stop holding the data.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top is always the latest revision. Significant changes will be called out where the community gathers; continuing to use the services after a revision means you accept the updated policy.
